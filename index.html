Features
Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Designed to be read at a glance, mounted on your handlebar at 30 km/h.

⚡

Live Telemetry

Voltage, current, power, battery %, speed, ODO, and PAS level — pulled directly from your EKD01-BF display over Bluetooth. No calibration needed.

🚗

Rear-View Radar

Connects to Garmin Varia. See approaching cars before you hear them — distance, speed, and a haptic alert the moment one enters your zone.

🗺️

GPX Navigation

Import any route from Mapy.cz, Komoot, Strava, or Files. Turn-by-turn chimes. Direction View keeps the road ahead in frame.

⌚

Apple Watch

Glance at your wrist for battery, range, and cars-behind. Haptic patterns let you feel a vehicle approaching without lifting your eyes off the road.

❤️

Apple Health Sync

Each completed ride is saved as a cycling workout in Apple Health, with the full GPS route. Opt-in, on-device. Your data stays yours.

🤖

AI-Powered Battery & Range

VeloRadar reads live voltage directly from the controller and learns your riding patterns over time. The more you ride, the more accurate your battery % and remaining range become — personalised to your specific battery, terrain, and style.

🌤️

Weather Forecast

Current temperature and the next 12 hours via Apple WeatherKit — right in the top bar, updated as you move.

🎨

Light & Dark Themes

Dark for night rides (neon dashboard), Light for harsh daylight (calm readable colors), or follow the system automatically.

📊

Trip Statistics

Distance, time, avg/max speed, energy (Wh), power, elevation, temperature, PAS split — for the current ride and the last 10 trips.
