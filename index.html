Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Big numbers, minimal chrome - readable at a glance from your handlebar, even at speed.

Live battery & range

Live voltage from the controller calculated against a real 13S Li-ion discharge curve. 84% actually means 84%. Range adapts to your riding time, energy, and power use - not a fixed estimate.

Live telemetry

Voltage, current, power, speed, ODO, TRIP, PAS level - pulled directly from your EKD01-BF over Bluetooth. No calibration. No companion app. Identical to your bike's display.

Rear-view radar

Works with any Garmin Varia radar. See cars approaching before you hear them - distance, speed, and a haptic alert when one enters your alert zone.

GPX navigation

Import any route from Mapy.cz, Komoot, Strava, or Files. Turn-by-turn chimes. Direction View keeps the road ahead in frame; one tap shows the whole route flat.

Apple Watch

Glance at your wrist for battery, range, and cars-behind alerts. Haptic patterns let you feel when a vehicle approaches - so you can shoulder-check when it matters.

Apple Health sync

Each completed ride is saved as a cycling workout in Apple Health, with the full GPS route. Opt-in, on-device. Your data stays yours.

Weather forecast

Current temperature and the next 12 hours via Apple WeatherKit - right in the top bar, updated as you move. No third-party weather APIs.

Light & dark themes

Dark for night rides (neon dashboard), Light for harsh daylight (calm readable colors), or follow the system automatically. Tuned for handlebar readability.

Trip statistics

Distance, time, avg/max speed, energy (Wh), power, elevation, temperature, PAS split - for the current ride and the last 10 trips. Export any as GPX.

Compatibility

with EKD01-BF
