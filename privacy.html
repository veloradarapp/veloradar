Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Applies to VeloRadar for iOS

In one sentence: VeloRadar has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising SDKs. We don't have a copy of your rides — only you do.

Bike & Radar Data

🚲

Bike & radar telemetry

Battery, speed, distance, motor data, and Garmin Varia radar threats are read over Bluetooth and stay on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.

Location

📍

GPS

Your location is used in real time for navigation, road grade, speed context, weather lookup, and ride history. It is never sent to us, because we have no servers.

🔒

Screen-locked tracking

Always Location is requested only if you turn on Settings → Ride → Track with screen locked. It keeps the route, trip stats, Watch context, and Live Activity updated while the phone is locked. You can turn it off in VeloRadar or revoke it in iOS Settings.

Weather

🌤️

Apple WeatherKit

When weather is fetched, your approximate location is sent to Apple's WeatherKit service. Apple's privacy policy applies. No other weather provider is contacted.

Maps

🗺️

Apple Maps (default)

Map rendering goes through Apple Maps. Apple's privacy policy applies.

🌍

OpenStreetMap / CyclOSM / OpenTopoMap (optional)

If you switch the map source in Settings, tiles are loaded from the corresponding public OSM tile server. Your IP and approximate location are visible to that server, as with any web map.

Apple Health

❤️

Apple Health (opt-in)

If you turn on Settings → Ride → Sync rides to Apple Health, each completed ride is saved as a cycling workout with its GPS route. Data is written to your device's Health database; nothing is sent to us. Off by default. Revoke in iOS Settings → Privacy → Health → VeloRadar.

Storage

💾

On-device only

Trip history, imported routes, settings, and paired-device IDs are stored locally on your iPhone. They are included in your encrypted iCloud or Finder backup. Uninstalling VeloRadar removes them.

📤

Export

You can export individual rides as GPX files at any time via the iOS share sheet. VeloRadar never uploads them.

What we don't do

🚫

No analytics

We do not embed Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, Sentry, or any other tracking SDK. VeloRadar makes no network calls beyond the weather and map sources listed above.

🚫

No advertising

No ads, no ad networks, no ad identifiers (IDFA).

🚫

No accounts

VeloRadar has no sign-in, no account creation, no email collection.

Contact

Questions? Write to veloradar.app@gmail.com. This document is also in the app under Settings → Legal & About → Privacy.
