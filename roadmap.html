What's next

Shipping now

v1.0.2 - First public release

Everything described on the homepage. In final field-testing before App Store submission. See full changelog →

Next

v1.1 - AI-powered battery learning

Personalised battery percentage and range estimation using on-device machine learning. After ~10-20 rides, VeloRadar will start adapting the discharge curve to your specific battery's behaviour - accounting for cell aging, temperature, and your individual riding patterns.

  • Core ML model trained on-device - no data leaves your phone
  • Continuously improves accuracy the more you ride
  • Refined range estimation factoring in elevation, temperature, and wind
  • Optional "show confidence interval" mode for transparency
Later

v1.2 - Simple View & ride coaching

A fourth page in the app: a stripped-down Simple View with just four giant numbers (speed, battery%, power, range). Designed to be readable from a metre away - and to double as the honest hero screenshot for this website.

  • Simple View - minimal one-glance dashboard
  • Ride coaching: "you're 5km from your usual turnaround, battery at 42%"
  • Crash detection via accelerometer + automatic SOS to Apple's emergency services
  • iCloud sync for trip history archive (opt-in)
Someday

Beyond

Bigger ideas we'd love to do but aren't priorities yet:

  • Independent watchOS app - track a ride from the Watch alone, without iPhone
  • Strava direct upload after rides (currently you can share the GPX manually)
  • Off-route re-routing via Mapy.cz API
  • Support for additional Bafang displays - APT 580C/600C/750C/860C and DPC181 - if a Bafang owner with one of those displays shares a Bluetooth capture from the official app
  • Voice navigation for hands-free turn cues
