Frequently asked questions

🔌

My bike won't connect

Make sure your EKD01-BF display is powered on and nearby. The top bar shows the current status. If stuck on "Connecting…", try the Retry button or force-quit and reopen the app. iPhone Bluetooth must be on (Settings → Bluetooth).

🚗

Garmin Varia not connecting

Make sure the Varia is powered on (one press of the power button). If it hasn't been used for 10+ minutes it may have auto-slept and powered off — press the button again to wake it. The radar icon in the top bar shows the connection status.

🗺️

Route not importing from Mapy.cz / Komoot / Strava

In Mapy.cz, open your route → Share → VeloRadar. In Komoot or Strava, export the route as GPX then open the file with VeloRadar via Files or your email. The file must be a valid .gpx format.

🌤️

Weather not showing

VeloRadar uses Apple WeatherKit, which requires a valid GPS fix. Wait a few minutes after launch for a location lock. Make sure Location permission is granted (Settings → Privacy → Location → VeloRadar → While Using).

❤️

Workout not appearing in Apple Health

After completing a ride, tap Reset TRIP on the Stats screen. The workout is sent to Health at that moment. Make sure the Sync toggle is on (Settings → Ride → Sync rides to Apple Health) and iOS Health permission is granted (iOS Settings → Privacy → Health → VeloRadar → turn on Fitness).

🔋

Battery % seems off

Make sure the correct battery type is selected in Settings → Ride → Battery Type. For most Bafang BBS02 builds with a 48V pack, this should be "48V Li-ion (13S)" (54.0 V = 100%, 42 V = 0%).

📍

Trip tracking stops when I lock my screen

Enable Settings → Ride → Track with screen locked. iOS will ask for "Always" location permission — this keeps the GPS, trip stats, and route recording active while the screen is off.

⚙️

Where are the old Diagnostics?

Raw BLE diagnostics were removed in v1.0.2. If you need low-level device info (firmware version, hardware revision, UUID), check Settings → Support Details → Bike Display.

Still need help?

Email us at support@veloradar.app with your iPhone model, iOS version, and the problem you're seeing. Screenshots and a short description of steps to reproduce help a lot.
